Delete Your Luca Account

Luca by The Devvy Dev

Last updated: March 9, 2026

You can request deletion of your Luca app account and the data associated with it using either method below.

Option 1: Delete in the app

  1. Open Luca and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

This removes your session on the device and clears local app data. We also process deletion of your cloud-stored account data.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, email us from the address tied to your Luca account:

Request account deletion

Or email admin@thedevvydev.com with the subject line Delete my Luca account and include the email address you used to sign up.

What we delete

When your account is deleted, we delete data associated with your account, including:

  • Your account and sign-in information
  • Your style profile and analysis results
  • Your wardrobe items, outfits, and closet photos stored for your account

Subscriptions

Deleting your Luca account does not automatically cancel an active subscription billed through the Apple App Store or Google Play.

Please cancel your subscription before or after requesting account deletion:

  • iOS: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → Luca
  • Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Luca

Data we may retain

We may retain limited information where required by law or for legitimate business needs, such as fraud prevention, security, or tax and payment record-keeping. Any retained data is kept only as long as necessary for those purposes.

How long deletion takes

In-app deletion begins immediately on your device. Cloud account deletion is typically completed within 30 days of your request. Email requests are processed after we verify that you own the account.
